# Clone Booth

Prop an iPad up so it stays still, take several photos of yourself standing in different spots, and every copy of you appears in one picture. Tap a body to turn it into a ghost, censor it, glitch it, make it a hologram and more. Drag a body to move it.

Open it at https://craigwrenasmir.github.io/clone-booth/ in Safari on an iPad (or any phone or computer with a camera). Everything runs in the browser; no photos leave the device.

Tips: shoot the "Empty scene" first with nobody in it for the cleanest cut-outs, keep the lighting steady, and use the Cut-out slider if too much or too little shows.
