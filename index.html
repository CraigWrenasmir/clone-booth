How to make clones

  1. Prop the iPad up so it does not move at all. A stand, a shelf or a pile of books works.
  2. Tap Empty scene and step out of the picture. This teaches the app what the room looks like without you.
  3. Pick a timer, tap the big Shoot button, and get into the picture. Do it again standing somewhere new each time. Series ×4 takes four photos in a row so you can hop between spots.
  4. Go to Edit. Every copy of you appears in one picture. Tap a body to choose an effect: ghost, censor, glitch, hologram and more. Drag a body to move it.
  5. Tap Save photo to keep your creation.

Tips: keep the lighting steady, wear clothes that are a different colour from the wall, and use the Cut-out slider if too much or too little of you is showing. No empty scene? Take at least three photos and the app will work it out.
